Trigger, then gate.
Never a black box.

Other tools ask a model if an ad “feels” risky. We require a pattern to match, then a qualifier can clear it. Gemini may paint the display verdict. It does not decide the hit.

  1. Ingest — text as-is; images become a structured VLM packet (claims, OCR, objects).
  2. Trigger — a pack pattern must match: “clinically proven,” “2× gentler,” “biodegradable.”
  3. Gate — a nearby disclosure can license the hit. Absolute packs (e.g. disease cures) never clear.
  4. Audit — clause, quote, trigger, and licensed_by stay on the graph.
Licensed · clear Qualifier is in range. The hit stays on the graph as licensed_by. The flight can move.
Absolute · fail Disease cures and similar packs never clear. Fail, with the clause attached.
Borderline · ReviewLens Gated miss or both-twins. A person records approve / needs-changes / reject. That decision is the record.

Compliance says what you can’t say.
Virality Index says whether it’s worth saying.

One paste, two independent reads. The compliance verdict stays deterministic and cited. The Virality Index is an advisory creative score on seven dimensions taken from the sharing research — scored separately, never able to move a compliance outcome.

Seven dimensions, not a vibe

  • Arousal — high-activation emotion of any kind. Anger and anxiety travel as far as delight; calm positivity doesn’t.
  • Social currency — does sharing it make the sharer look good.
  • Practical value — useful enough that passing it on helps someone.
  • Story — a narrative carries it, rather than a flat assertion.
  • Novelty — it violates an expectation the reader already holds.
  • Triggers — tied to a recurring cue, so it resurfaces on its own.
  • Observability — the result is visible and easy to imitate.

After Berger & Milkman (2012) on arousal, and Berger’s STEPPS constructs.

Two things we refuse to fold into the score

Clarity is comprehension hygiene, not a sharing driver — so it’s reported as a diagnostic and flagged when it’s low enough to cap everything else.

Brand prominence measurably reduces sharing, so rewarding it would be backwards. It’s surfaced as a warning instead of being quietly averaged in.

Equal weights, on purpose

All seven dimensions carry the same weight. With no outcome data of your own, invented coefficients are false precision — and equal weights are hard to beat with guessed ones. The payload says uniform_prior so you always know which it is.

Fit them on your own ad performance later, and the score becomes yours.
